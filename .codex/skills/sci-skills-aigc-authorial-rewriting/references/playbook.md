# AIGC Risk and Authorial Rewriting Playbook

## Objective

Improve authentic authorship, scientific specificity, evidence alignment, and readable variation. Do not optimize for detector evasion, call a detector, estimate a guaranteed score, add random errors, or fabricate personal experience.

## Risk map

Audit generic background, uniform cadence, empty transitions, repetitive templates, inflated novelty or causality, abstract nouns hiding actions, weak evidence density, terminology drift, missing methodological decisions, and polished prose masking gaps. Label issues `STYLE`, `AUTHOR_INPUT`, `EVIDENCE`, `LOGIC`, or `TERMINOLOGY` with priority.

## Rewrite sequence

1. Lock facts, numbers, citations, terminology, statistical meaning, uncertainty, and causal strength.
2. Identify each paragraph's real argumentative function.
3. Ask for genuine author decisions, alternatives, unexpected findings, limitations, and interpretation when missing.
4. Replace generic prose with verified choices, evidence, contrasts, conditions, and limitations.
5. Compare original and revision for meaning and traceability.

Return the risk map, author questions, clean revision, original/revised/reason table, locked-fact ledger, terminology ledger, and meaning-preservation audit. A supplied detector report is only a fallible signal.
