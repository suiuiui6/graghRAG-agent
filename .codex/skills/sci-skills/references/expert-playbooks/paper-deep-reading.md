# Paper Deep Reading Playbook

## Source-depth gate

Record whether the source is full text with figures and supplements, publisher page, preprint, abstract, excerpt, or author notes. Abstract-only evidence supports an abstract analysis, not a full-paper reconstruction.

## Reading depths

- `QUICK`: position, question, method, main finding, and relevance.
- `STANDARD`: complete experiment-to-claim chain and limitations.
- `DEEP`: module logic, formulas, assumptions, experiment matrix, conclusion boundaries, connections, and testable ideas.

## Sixteen-section card

Bibliographic position; research question; background route; unresolved pain point; core insight; method overview; module or mechanism logic; formulas and assumptions; data or material; design, metrics, and baselines; experiment-to-claim table; conclusion boundary; author-stated limitations; critical assessment; reusable connections; testable follow-up ideas.

For formulas define every symbol, input, output, assumption, role, and failure condition. For each idea state the unresolved problem, difference from the source, evidence required, falsifiable result, minimum viable version, and main risk. Do not call an idea novel without broader literature verification.

Use source locators such as page, section, figure, table, equation, or supplement. Separate `SOURCE_FACT`, `EXTERNAL_VERIFICATION`, `AI_ANALYSIS`, and `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`.
