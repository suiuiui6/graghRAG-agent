# Paper Presentation Playbook

## Story before slides

Classify the source and build an evidence arc: importance, gap, study action, method or mechanism, results supporting each claim, genuine contribution, uncertainty, and audience takeaway. Every slide gets one purpose, one headline claim, supporting evidence, and a transition.

## Deliverables

- audience, purpose, duration, and language brief;
- slide map with headline, evidence, figure, and notes;
- figure provenance and locator table;
- complete speaker notes;
- actual PPTX and rendered QA when local tools are available;
- otherwise a complete production blueprint and manual software companion.

Preserve source attribution, captions, labels, scale bars, legends, and statistical marks. Do not imply stronger evidence or higher resolution than the source provides. Check overflow, alignment, crop, font size, contrast, density, terminology, attribution, and story coherence.
