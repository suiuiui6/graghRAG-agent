# Pre-submission Review Playbook

## Fact base

Extract paper family, article type, target if known, central claims, contribution, data or materials, methods, validation, key results, figures, limitations, missing sections, unavailable supplements, and unverified journal requirements.

## Three independent lenses

1. `CONTRIBUTION`: originality, importance, positioning, and audience fit.
2. `METHODS`: design, data, statistics, robustness, reproducibility, and claim fit.
3. `COMMUNICATION`: argument, figures, terminology, transparency, accessibility, and package completeness.

For every concern give manuscript location, evidence, consequence, severity (`BLOCKER`, `MAJOR`, `MODERATE`, `MINOR`), minimum repair, and ideal repair. Synthesize shared blockers, disagreements, high-risk claims, missing validation, effort, and readiness as `READY`, `CONDITIONALLY_READY`, or `NOT_READY`.

Do not impersonate a named reviewer, predict an editorial decision, or invent experiments as mandatory journal requirements.
