# Manuscript Writing Playbook

## Evidence before prose

Inventory the research question, approved contribution, supporting results or sources, allowed causal strength, target audience, article type, and missing evidence. Create a claim ledger before drafting result-bearing prose.

## Section contracts

- Title: concrete problem, object, and contribution without promotional claims.
- Abstract: problem, gap, method, evidence, result, and bounded contribution.
- Introduction: context, unresolved problem, precise gap, approach, and contribution.
- Related work: synthesis by problem, mechanism, method, or evidence rather than paper-by-paper narration.
- Methods: reproducible data, design, modules, parameters, analysis, and validation.
- Results: observations first; interpretation only when signposted and supported.
- Discussion: meaning, literature comparison, alternatives, limitations, and implications.
- Conclusion: answer the question without new evidence.

## Figure placement

For every proposed figure identify the exact manuscript location, purpose, content, evidence role, required material, overview-versus-module decomposition, and generation route. Do not render without the scientific-figure gate and user approval.

Use `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED` rather than filling missing results, citations, novelty, methods, ethics, or journal requirements. Preserve numbers, units, entities, statistical meaning, uncertainty, validation scope, and association-versus-causation.
