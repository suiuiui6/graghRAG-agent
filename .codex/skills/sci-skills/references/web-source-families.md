# Web Source Families for Humanities and Social Sciences

Use this guide to generate topic-specific source choices. Verify the actual dataset, current access, price, fields, and license from official pages before recommending it.

| Source family | Typical research evidence | Prefer before crawling | Main cautions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Official statistics | Population, economy, education, labor, health, regions | Official table download or bulk file | Definition changes, revisions, aggregation |
| Administrative and open government data | Permits, budgets, institutions, public services, geographic records | Open-data portal or agency export | Coverage and policy-driven missingness |
| Survey and panel archives | Attitudes, behavior, demographics, longitudinal outcomes | Licensed microdata or institutional repository | Weighting, consent, access tier |
| Policy and legal sources | Laws, regulations, policy notices, consultation documents | Official gazette, ministry archive, document download | Version, effective date, jurisdiction |
| News and media archives | Events, frames, discourse, agenda and attention | Licensed archive, official feed, database export | Copyright, source imbalance, duplicate syndication |
| Digital humanities and cultural archives | Books, newspapers, images, performances, historical records | Library/archive download or catalogue export | Edition, OCR quality, quotation rights |
| Corporate and market disclosures | Reports, filings, prices, products, governance | Regulator or exchange filing, company download, licensed database | Survivorship, restatement, redistribution |
| Jobs and education opportunities | Job demand, skills, salaries, programs, admissions | Official statistics, licensed dataset, or manual platform export | Posting duplication, representativeness, platform rules |
| Geospatial and place data | Land use, facilities, mobility, neighborhoods | Downloadable GIS files or manual map export | Coordinate system, temporal mismatch, sensitive locations |
| Reviews and forums | Experience, satisfaction, complaints, community norms | Authorized dataset or permitted public-page collection | Self-selection, manipulation, identity and quotation risk |
| Social platforms | Public discourse, interaction, trends, networks | Licensed dataset, user export, or permitted public-page collection | Personal data, deletions, context collapse, unstable access |
| Search and trend data | Attention, issue salience, information seeking | Official trends interface or licensed manual export | Normalization, personalization, opaque sampling |

For each candidate, explain which part of the research question it can and cannot answer. A larger dataset is not automatically better than a smaller source with clearer constructs, coverage, rights, and provenance.
