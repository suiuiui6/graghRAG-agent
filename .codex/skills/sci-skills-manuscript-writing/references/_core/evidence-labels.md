# Evidence and Integrity Labels

Use these labels consistently:

- `VERIFIED_USER_MATERIAL`: directly supported by a supplied file, record, output, or explicit author confirmation.
- `VERIFIED_SOURCE`: checked against an accessible primary or official source with a locator and date when mutable.
- `EXECUTED_LOCAL`: produced by an actual local run whose inputs, command or source, and output are available.
- `AI_INFERENCE`: reasoned from verified material but not itself an observed fact.
- `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED`: depends on an author decision, experience, interpretation, or missing fact.
- `PENDING_VERIFICATION`: plausible but not yet checked at the required source.
- `NOT_EXECUTED`: planned code, analysis, rendering, deposit, export, or upload that has not run.

Never convert `AI_INFERENCE`, `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, or `NOT_EXECUTED` into verified evidence through polished wording. Preserve numbers, units, entities, citations, statistical meaning, uncertainty, and association-versus-causation boundaries.
