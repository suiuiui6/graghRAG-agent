# Reviewer Response Playbook

## Intake and tracking

Require the complete decision letter, every comment with stable IDs, current manuscript, completed-change evidence or author notes, and available journal instructions. Record request, category, severity, action, owner, status, evidence, manuscript location, response draft, and blocker for every comment.

## Response anatomy

1. Acknowledge and restate the issue accurately.
2. State the verified action or reasoned position.
3. Present supporting evidence.
4. Quote or summarize the revised text.
5. Give the exact manuscript location.

Never claim an experiment, reanalysis, edit, or upload without returned evidence. For infeasible requests, explain constraints, add the strongest feasible analysis or limitation, and narrow the claim. Resolve conflicting comments explicitly and disagree through evidence.

Deliver the tracker, point-by-point response, revision cover letter, change-location map, marked-text plan, unresolved author inputs, consistency audit, and manual submission companion when necessary.
