# Literature Search Playbook

## Intake

Capture the research question, evidence purpose, discipline, concepts, population or object, method, time range, language, document type, target databases, and access constraints. Distinguish reconnaissance, benchmark discovery, reproducible search, citation verification, and systematic-style screening.

## Execution

1. Build concept blocks containing controlled terms, synonyms, abbreviations, spelling variants, and cautious wildcards.
2. Produce a broad discovery query and a narrow confirmation query for each source.
3. Record source, exact query, date, filters, hit count, and access limitation.
4. Verify title, author, year, venue, DOI or identifier, article type, correction or retraction status, and relevance from an accessible primary record.
5. Deduplicate by DOI, normalized title, then author-year-title comparison.
6. Label relevance as `EXACT_AI_APPLICATION`, `ADJACENT_AI_APPLICATION`, `DOMAIN_ONLY`, `AI_ONLY`, or `TYPE_EXAMPLE`. For SCI-Skills topic recommendations, prioritize genuine AI plus application-domain studies.

## Deliverables

- concept and synonym table;
- reproducible source-specific search strings;
- verified candidate table with locators and access status;
- screening and exclusion log;
- deduplication summary;
- claim-to-reference evidence matrix;
- export/import guidance for RIS, BibTeX, NBIB, CSV, or PDF.

Do not call a search systematic without complete queries, dates, sources, filters, criteria, deduplication, and screening records. Never invent bibliographic fields or claim full-text verification from an abstract.
